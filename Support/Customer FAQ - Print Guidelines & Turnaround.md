## 1. Standard Production Turnaround Times

| Order Type | Standard Turnaround | Rush Available? | Rush Surcharge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Screen Print (under 500 pcs)** | **7 – 10 Business Days** | Yes (3–5 Days) | +25% |
| **High Volume (500 – 5,000 pcs)** | **10 – 14 Business Days** | Case-by-case | Inquire with Production Mgr |
| **Specialty (Discharge, Foil, Puff)** | **10 – 12 Business Days** | No | N/A |
| **Samples / Strike-Offs** | **3 – 5 Business Days** | N/A | $50 / screen fee |

> [!NOTE]
> Turnaround time begins *after* final mockup art approval and payment confirmation, NOT when the initial order was submitted.

## 2. Artwork File Requirements

For highest print quality, artwork files must meet these specifications:

- **Vector Formats (Preferred):** `.ai`, `.eps`, `.pdf` with all fonts converted to curves/outlines.
- **Raster Formats:** High-resolution `.psd` or `.png` at **300 DPI** at 100% final print size.
- **Color Standards:** Please provide target **Pantone Solid Coated (PMS C)** numbers. If RGB/CMYK only is provided, colors will be matched visually to the nearest Pantone swatch.
- **Minimum Line Weights:**
  - Positive print line width: **1.0 pt minimum** (0.014 in).
  - Reverse/knockout line width: **1.5 pt minimum** (prevents ink choke-out).
