# Formulas: Pantone 186C & Discharge Mixing Guide

**Department:** Press Room

## 1. Pantone 186C (Apex Crimson Red)

Standard formula for **1,000 grams (1 kg)** finished plastisol ink mix.

| Pigment Component | Concentration (%) | Weight for 1,000g Batch |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mixing White (Opaque)** | 4.2% | **42.0 g** |
| **Rubine Red** | 35.8% | **358.0 g** |
| **Warm Red / Scarlet** | 58.0% | **580.0 g** |
| **Black Concentrate** | 2.0% | **20.0 g** |
| **Total** | 100.0% | **1,000.0 g** |

### Press Guidelines for 186C:
- **Underbase:** Print flash print over 156-mesh Cotton White if printing on dark 100% cotton or 50/50.
- **Direct on Light:** Print directly through 230-mesh with a 70/90/70 triple durometer squeegee.
- **Cure Temperature:** Standard cure at **320°F (160°C)** for 45 seconds in the conveyor dryer chamber.

---

## 2. Water-Based Discharge Mixing Protocol

Discharge ink chemically bleaches the reactive dye out of 100% cotton garments and replaces it with pigment, producing a feather-soft hand feel.

### 1,000g Batch Formula:
- **Discharge Clear Base:** **850 g** (85%)
- **Color Pigment Concentrate:** **100 g** (10%)
- **Zinc Formaldehyde Sulfoxylate (ZFS) Activator:** **50 g** (5.0% by weight)

:::caution
Once the ZFS activator is mixed into the discharge base, pot life is **6 to 8 hours max**. Only mix what you will print in the current shift!
:::


### Dryer Curing Specs for Discharge:
- Conveyor chamber temperature: **340°F (171°C)**
- Minimum dwell time: **90 seconds** (evaporation of water is required before the discharge reaction triggers).
