## Why This Architecture Wins

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Obsidian Floor Velocity" icon="pencil">
		Screen print managers and lead printers edit notes at lightning speed in Markdown with full offline support. No web forms or bloated intranet lag.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Git Automated Cloud Sync" icon="random">
		Changes are automatically version-controlled and pushed silently every 5 minutes. No more duplicate corrupted copies (`Note (1).md`) from Dropbox or OneDrive.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Darkroom Codespaces & Copilot" icon="laptop">
		Standing by the wash-out sink or press with an iPad or phone? Open the repository in GitHub Codespaces with Copilot Business for instant AI troubleshooting.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Instant Front-of-House Portal" icon="rocket">
		Customer support, sales, and front desk access a blazing-fast, searchable web manual without needing Obsidian or technical training.
	</Card>
