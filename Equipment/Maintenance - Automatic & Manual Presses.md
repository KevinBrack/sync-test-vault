**Department:** Production & Maintenance  
**Applies To:** Lead Press Operator, Maintenance Tech

## 1. Daily Startup & Shutdown Checklist (Automatic Press)

### Morning Startup:
- [ ] Drain water trap on the central air compressor line.
- [ ] Inspect main air regulator: target **90–100 PSI (6.2–6.9 bar)**.
- [ ] Wipe down print heads and squeegee carriage rails with lint-free shop rag.
- [ ] Verify safety cords and emergency stop buttons around carousel perimeter.
- [ ] Inspect flash cure units for lint buildup on quartz lamps or ceramic elements.

### End-of-Shift Shutdown:
- [ ] Remove all excess ink from screens and return to labeled containers.
- [ ] Clean squeegees, flood bars, and squeegee holders thoroughly with press wash.
- [ ] Raise all print heads into the locked service position.
- [ ] Power down flash cure units and let cooling fans run for 5 minutes before main switch cutoff.
- [ ] Sweep platen lint and apply pallet paper replacement if needed for tomorrow's run.

---

## 2. Weekly & Monthly Preventive Schedule

| Frequency | Component | Action Required | Lubricant / Spec |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly** | Squeegee carriage linear rails | Clean and apply light machine oil | ISO VG 32 or 3-in-One Oil |
| **Weekly** | Pneumatic lubricator bowls | Inspect oil level in bowl; refill if low | Non-detergent air tool oil |
| **Monthly** | Carousel indexing wheel | Grease main index pins and cam followers | NLGI #2 Lithium grease |
| **Monthly** | Flash cure quartz tubes | Check continuity & inspect wiring terminals | Clean with 99% isopropyl alcohol |
